Design Guide

Do's & Don'ts of Sign Design

Practical tips we share with every client before we begin — these will make your signage more effective.

Do

  • Keep it legible from a distance

    Match letter height to viewing distance — 1" of letter height per 10 ft is a good baseline.

  • Use high-contrast colors

    Dark backgrounds with light letters — or vice versa — read best at night.

  • Stick to 2 fonts max

    One for the brand name, one for supporting text. More feels cluttered.

  • Plan for night visibility

    Review the mockup in a dark scene before approving the final render.

  • Match signage to architecture

    Modern building? Go trimless. Vintage storefront? Classic channel letters work best.

Don't

  • Overcrowd the sign

    Avoid cramming phone, address, tagline and services on one panel.

  • Mix too many colors

    3+ neon colors on the same sign dilutes focus — pick a hero color.

  • Ignore local signage codes

    Some districts limit brightness or sign size — we check before designing.

  • Use thin scripts at small sizes

    Hairline fonts lose legibility — choose bolder for window pieces.

  • Skimp on installation planning

    Consider power access, mounting surface and viewing angles from the start.

Design FAQ

Questions Before We Start

Common questions about our design and mockup process, answered clearly.

Do I need to provide a design file?
No — a logo, rough sketch or even a written idea is enough for us to start. We'll convert it into a clean vector and full 3D mockup.

How many revisions are included?
Every project includes two rounds of revisions at the concept stage and one final revision after the 3D mockup — all free of charge.

Can I see the sign in my actual space?
Yes. Send us a photo of your storefront or wall, and we'll composite a realistic mockup with the sign in place — day and night versions included.

What if I don't like the mockup?
We don't move to fabrication until you're 100% happy. If the first concept isn't right, we'll explore new directions — no pressure and no extra fee.

Can you match my exact brand colors?
Absolutely. Share your brand guide or a Pantone / HEX reference, and we'll use calibrated LED modules and acrylics to match as closely as physically possible.

Ready to design your sign?

Send us a photo of your space and a few details — we'll come back with a free mockup and quote.
