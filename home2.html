The Design & Build Process

Getting a custom illuminated sign for your business is simple. We handle everything from the initial artwork to the final wiring.

1

Upload Logo & Enquire

Send us your logo, font, or ideas along with rough dimensions. Our team reviews your requirements.

2

Free Digital Mockup

We provide a free visual mockup showing exactly how the sign will look illuminated, plus a detailed quote.

3

Precision Fabrication

Once approved, our technicians CNC-route the backing and hand-shape the LED tubing to your exact specifications.

4

Delivery & Install

We securely package your sign for safe delivery, complete with a plug-and-play kit or hardwiring instructions.
