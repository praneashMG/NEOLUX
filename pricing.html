What Affects Price

Five Factors That Shape Your Quote

Understanding what drives cost helps you plan smarter. Here's exactly how we price custom signage.

Size & Scale

Larger signs use more materials, LEDs and structural framing.

Color Complexity

Single-color costs less than RGB or multi-zone addressable LEDs.

Indoor vs Outdoor

Weather-rated IP65 builds need sealed housings and marine hardware.

Installation

Height, wall material and permit needs affect install labor.

Timeline

Rush orders within 7 days include a small priority fee.

Pricing FAQ

Common Pricing Questions

Everything you need to know about how we quote, invoice, and deliver your signage project.

How is my quote calculated?
We price based on size, materials, LED type, mounting complexity and installation. You'll receive a fixed-price quote with a line-by-line breakdown before we begin — no surprises.

Do you require a deposit?
Yes. We take a 50% deposit to begin production, with the remaining balance due on completion. Enterprise projects can be split into milestone payments.

Is installation included?
Installation is quoted separately based on your location and mounting needs, starting from $199 for standard indoor jobs. Our team handles everything from wiring to final cleanup.

Do you offer bulk discounts?
Absolutely. Orders of 3+ identical signs or multi-location rollouts receive 10–20% volume discounts. Contact us for a custom bulk quote.

What's your refund policy?
Because every sign is custom-made, deposits are non-refundable once production starts. However, if a sign arrives defective, we repair or replace it free under warranty.
